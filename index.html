About

Pari (Bad Soden am Taunus, 1993) is a Berlin-based cultural worker, writer, musician, and singer with over a decade of experience in arts and cultural management. She holds a Bachelor of Music specializing in pedagogy from McMaster University (Hamilton, Canada, 2015) and a Graduate Certificate in Arts and Cultural Management from Humber College (Toronto, Canada, 2018).

Driven by a commitment to creating inclusive, postmigrant spaces, Pari brings people together through arts and culture, with a particular focus on making room for voices and perspectives that are often overlooked. Her work spans international cultural programming, communications, partnership development, and grant management. In Canada, she led a nationwide initiative that connected over 17,000 young people with the film industry. She has also worked with the Goethe-Institut Toronto and Goethe-Institut im Exil in Berlin. Currently, she works with Vokalhelden e.V., organizing accessible choral programming and performances for children and young people across Berlin.

In 2026, Pari co-founded and co-curated Sarkash سرکش Underground Festival, an independent music festival in Berlin celebrating underground trap, rap, and experimental music from Afghanistan, Balochistan, and Kashmir. The festival brought together international artists and 650 attendees for live performances, DJ sets, and workshops.

Alongside her work in cultural management, Pari continues her artistic practice as a singer and facilitator, developing participatory vocal workshops that invite people to explore their voices in playful and unexpected ways. Throughout her career, she has moved between organizing and making art, creating spaces where people can express themselves and feel a sense of belonging.

Vocal Activation: Voice, Movement & Rhythm

Drawing on her background as a singer, musician, and music educator, Pari develops participatory workshops that invite people to explore their voices in a playful and supportive environment. Through vocal exercises, movement, rhythm, and improvisation, participants are encouraged to experiment with sound and discover new ways of expressing themselves. No previous singing or musical experience is required. Pari is available to facilitate workshops independently or for workplaces, organizations, and community groups. Workshops can be adapted to different settings and participants. For inquiries and collaborations, please visit the Contact section below or email hellopariludin@gmail.com.

Resonant Bodies @ Sarkash سرکش Underground Festival (August 2026, Berlin)

Developed and facilitated a 90-minute vocal workshop exploring the relationship between voice, movement, and the body. Through playful exercises and collective experimentation, participants were invited to explore vocal expression in a supportive environment.

Voice, Movement & Rhythm @ BIWOC* Rising (October 2025, Berlin)

Developed and facilitated an interactive workshop as part of BIWOC* Rising's 3 C's Cohort, exploring voice, movement, and rhythm through collective exercises and improvisation. Created a welcoming space for participants to experiment with their voices and connect through shared artistic practice.
